# HORIZON

## Managed Home Tuition Platform

> **The Right Tutor. For Your Child.**

Horizon is a local managed home-tuition service that connects parents with suitable tutors for Classes **5–12**, while Horizon manages the complete tuition experience — from enquiry and student assessment to tutor matching, scheduling, progress tracking, feedback, and tutor replacement.

The goal is not to become a simple "teacher listing website".

The goal is to build a **trusted local education brand** where parents purchase a managed tuition service from Horizon and tutors work through the Horizon network.

---

# 1. The Problem

Parents often face several problems while searching for home tuition:

* Finding a reliable tutor is difficult.
* Parents don't know whether a tutor is actually suitable for their child.
* They don't know the tutor's teaching quality beforehand.
* Finding another tutor when the current tutor is unavailable is difficult.
* Parents have to independently negotiate fees and schedules.
* There is usually no proper assessment of the student's academic weaknesses.
* There is no structured progress tracking.
* If a tutor leaves, the parent has to start searching again.

On the other side, tutors face:

* Difficulty finding genuine students.
* Irregular student leads.
* Spending time searching for tuition opportunities.
* Difficulty building credibility with new parents.
* No professional identity or organised student-management system.

---

# 2. Horizon's Solution

Horizon acts as a **managed tuition service** between parents and tutors.

Instead of simply saying:

> "Here is a teacher. Talk to them."

Horizon manages the entire process.

### Horizon Process

```text
Parent Enquiry
      ↓
Requirement Collection
      ↓
Student Assessment
      ↓
Tutor Matching
      ↓
Trial / Introduction
      ↓
Regular Home Tuition
      ↓
Progress Monitoring
      ↓
Parent Feedback
      ↓
Tutor Support / Replacement
```

The parent relationship remains with **Horizon**, while the tutor focuses primarily on teaching.

# 5. Business Model

## Important Principle

Horizon should avoid presenting itself as a simple commission marketplace.

Example of a weak model:

```text
Parent pays ₹5,000
        ↓
Tutor gets ₹4,000
        ↓
Horizon keeps ₹1,000
```

This creates a strong incentive for parent and tutor to bypass Horizon.

They may think:

> "Why should we pay Horizon? Let's deal directly."

That can destroy recurring revenue.

---

# 6. Horizon's Managed-Service Model

Instead, Horizon sells a **tuition package/service** to the parent.

Example:

```text
Parent
   ↓
HORIZON TUITION PLAN
   ↓
Horizon manages the tuition
   ↓
Tutor delivers teaching
```

The parent pays Horizon for the complete managed service.

The tutor receives compensation from Horizon according to their engagement terms.

### Example

Parent Package:

**Horizon Home Tuition — ₹5,000/month**

Horizon internally manages:

* Tutor compensation
* Student management
* Assessment
* Parent communication
* Tutor coordination
* Progress monitoring
* Replacement support
* Administrative operations

The exact tutor compensation should be based on:

* Class
* Subject
* Number of sessions
* Tutor experience
* Location
* Travel requirements
* Difficulty of the assignment

Therefore, Horizon is not locked into a fixed ₹1,000 commission.

# 8. Anti-Bypass Strategy

## The Problem

The biggest risk is:

```text
Parent ←→ Tutor
       ↓
They know each other
       ↓
Direct deal
       ↓
Horizon loses the student
```

Horizon cannot realistically prevent a parent and tutor from knowing each other.

Therefore, the strategy should NOT be:

> "Hide the tutor's identity."

Instead, the strategy is:

> **Make Horizon valuable enough that both sides prefer staying within Horizon.**
